Computing Pioneer

Mark Zuckerberg

1984 – Present • Entrepreneur & Computer Scientist

Mark Zuckerberg is an American media magnate, internet entrepreneur, and computer programmer. He is the co-founder and CEO of Meta (formerly Facebook), fundamentally changing how people across the globe communicate and connect digitally.

Key Milestones

  1. 1984: Born in White Plains, New York, USA.
  2. 2004: Launched the social network TheFacebook while at Harvard University.
  3. 2012: Led Facebook through its initial public offering (IPO) on the NASDAQ stock exchange.
  4. 2021: Rebranded Facebook Inc. to Meta to focus on building the metaverse.

Major Inventions

  • The Facebook Social Network
  • Metaverse Platform (Meta Horizon Worlds, Quest headsets)
  • Meta Product Ecosystem (acquisition and development of Instagram and WhatsApp)
